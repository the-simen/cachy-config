# Noctalia templates

## Noctalia custom palettes

Palettes dir: `$HOME/.config/noctalia/templates`

1. mpv: mpv-modernz.conf
2. niri: niri-palette.conf (instead of built-in from noctalia)
3. nvim: nvim-noctalia.lua (instead of the community one, mine works better)
4. superfile: superfile-palette.toml
5. telegram [^1]: 
    - telegram-contrast.tdesktop-theme (instead of the community one, mine works much better)
    - telegram-pack.py (generates solid background and packs the final theme zip)
6. tmux: tmux-palette.conf (instead of the community one)
7. discord [^1]: vesktop-colors-only.css (with original UI, the community one is too cringey)

[^1]: These palettes need to be installed manually
#### Telegram:
1. Go to Settings -> Chat Settings -> Chat wallpaper
2. Choose "Choose from file" and select the generated theme:
   `$HOME/.config/telegram-desktop/themes/noctalia-contrast.tdesktop-theme`
#### Discord:
1. Install vesktop from the CachyOS repository, or vesktop/vencord from wherever you want
2. Go to Settings -> Themes -> Local Themes
3. Choose "Noctalia Colors Only"
